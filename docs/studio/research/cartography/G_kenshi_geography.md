# G. Kenshi's geography: why each place looks the way it does (research, 22 Nov 2026)

Asked by George (22 Nov 2026), to learn how Kenshi decides what a region looks like, so our world can do the same from
its own history. **Limits:** the Kenshi fandom wiki, kenshi.wiki.gg and the Wayback Machine all refused automated reads
(HTTP 402). Fandom wording comes from search extracts. Most detail is from **kenshi.zone**, a fan wiki that separates
game data and quoted in-game text from speculation; its pages read like machine translation, so treat it as a good fan
source, not canon. Developer statements are from Chris Hunt's 2019 AMA and a Rock Paper Shotgun interview.

Labels: **[S]** stated in game text, dialogue, books or by the developer · **[I]** strongly implied by what the game shows ·
**[F]** fan theory · **[–]** no cause given anywhere.

## G1. How the developer built the map

- **G1.1.** Hunt (RPS): "We've tried to lay everything out logically, based on the story and the physics that'd occur. So
  you've got a volcano zone, where the water is acidic and there's continually falling ash." [S]
- **G1.2.** "Sometimes the entire feature of a zone might be that it's empty, desolate and lifeless"; "designing the map as
  laying traps for the player… a zone full of treasure… in the far reaches." [S]
- **G1.3.** Hunt "is not a fan of exposition": clues such as a possible meteor crater are left for players to read [S].
  Most "why" answers in Kenshi are [I] or [F]; the only explanations are a few Skeleton lines, books and map text.

Ancient-war scars cluster in two places: the north-west (Obedience) and the eastern belt (Venge, the Eye, Stobe's Garden).
Robots live where organics can't (the poisoned south-east and centre) and are absent from natural regions.

## G3. Reasons a place exists or looks as it does (about 70 zones)

| # | Reason | Share | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| G3.1 | Ancient war machine, wreck or weapon still running | ~8% | Venge, the Eye, Obedience, Stobe's Garden, Purple Sands |
| G3.2 | Ancient industry, pollution, labs | ~12% | Deadlands, Iron Valleys, Floodlands, the Grid, the Crater |
| G3.3 | Dead civilisation buried in the land | ~3 zones, and a ruin pin in ~60% of zones | Ashlands, Stenn's rebar, Mourn |
| G3.4 | Natural hazard or geology | ~15% | Volcanic zones, the Swamp, Fog Islands, craters |
| G3.5 | Animals or plants define it | ~15% | Leviathan Coast, Gut, Spider Plains, Bonefields |
| G3.6 | A faction settled on a resource or strong ground | ~20% | Admag on its hill, Stack's iron mines, the Great Desert's ore |
| G3.7 | Refuge or no-man's-land | ~8 zones | The Swamp, Hidden Forest, Shem; the Skeleton city behind its pollution |
| G3.8 | Front line or present-day war scar | ~4 zones | Bast, the Border Zone |
| G3.9 | Faith or cult | points | A machine worshipped as "the King" |
| G3.10 | Trade route or waystation | points | Grey Desert road, waystations |

About a quarter of zones take their look from an ancient catastrophe or technology; three quarters look as they do for
natural, faunal or political reasons, though most still hold a ruin.

## G4. How Kenshi avoids repeating its scars

- **G4.1.** Each signature scar appears once at full scale (one death ray, one crashed satellite, one Behemoth pit, one ash
  capital, one gas desert). Echoes elsewhere are small (a single Behemoth hand, satellite pieces in the sand).
- **G4.2.** Similar hazards behave differently: acid as rain, water or ground; gas that knocks out; beams by day only;
  fog and red rain that are only visual.
- **G4.3.** Even colour repeats are split: red water in two places only, craters in about three, each with a different
  occupant.
- **G4.4.** Robot remains are split by type: giants in one pit, human-sized wrecks in a workshop, spiders in the factory ruins.
- **G4.5.** The same kind of scar gets a different present-day user (a Skeleton city, Thrall Masters, a fortress over a lab).
- **G4.6.** The cause is usually withheld; one line of dialogue or an unreadable book stands in for exposition.
