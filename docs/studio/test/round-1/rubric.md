## 3. Rubric 

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Geography** — the result obeys the physical rules that read at map scale | Breaks a BLOCKING rule (water uphill, a lake not at the low point, a river with no end, a town with no water) | Plausible in parts; major rules unchecked | Rules checked with evidence; minor gaps | Every applicable physical rule checked with map evidence; departures stated with their play reason |
| **Fidelity** — follows the vault and the vault's decisions; invents no canon | Contradicts a vault decision or invents canon | Follows decisions but fills story gaps silently | Follows decisions; open items mostly marked | Every fact sourced; every gap OPEN with its question; bends only for stated play/look reasons |
| **Usefulness to the studio** — concrete, ranked, actionable in the consumers' terms | Vague advice | Some concrete fixes or facts | Ranked findings with ops, or handoff facts per zone | Ops ready to write; computed bearings; per-zone sheets in level-design and environment-art terms |
| **Craft and process** (0–1) | Hand edits, one era, no evidence | — | — | Ops not edits, both eras, before/after evidence, sources cited (scored 1) |

Score out of 10. A judge scores lower for a regression and lists every gap ranked and actionable.
