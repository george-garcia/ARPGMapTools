# Review: the checklist, the checks every pass runs, and the rubric

## 1. Region checklist (geography reviews and change requests)

Walk it in this order; each line names its rule. Gather evidence with reads (`query_cells`, `list`, layer screenshots,
`screenshot_3d`), not impressions.

**Form**
- [ ] The region is lopsided across the rift and long along its axis; margins are segmented; no box or frame-shaped rims (G1–G3).
- [ ] The escarpment is a stepped system with benches where the story needs shelves (G2); the plateau beyond stands above the land outside.
- [ ] Volcanoes and young basalt sit on or near the axis, or the departure is recorded (G4).
- [ ] The region blends into the continent at its edges (P17).

**Water**
- [ ] Every river ends at a sea, a lake or the basin's sink; none stops on open land without a note (G8, M7).
- [ ] Drainage designed per era: where each era's rivers end is intended, not generated by accident (M7).
- [ ] Closed lakes are salt or dry; no freshwater lake without an outlet unless fed and noted (G9).
- [ ] The pan is the lowest ground on land and on every water surface (G10, P19).
- [ ] Permanent water comes from a wetter source (plateau, springs on faults, the falls' catchment) (G11–G13).
- [ ] Lake count in the frame matches the intended pools, each with its subtype, group and name (azgaar-ops; M8, M11).

**Ground and climate**
- [ ] Ground runs coarse to fine downslope: gorge, fans, gravel, mud, gypsum, salt (G16–G18).
- [ ] Local things stay local: hydrothermal fields, springs, pools (G21–G22); the acid pits stand out as P20 asks.
- [ ] Every green place has a named water source (G25); the before era is wetter in its water, not only its paint (G26).
- [ ] Unpainted ground reads right under Azgaar's climate (M10).

**People**
- [ ] Every settlement sits on water and on usable ground (G31); routes take low passes and climb scarps by switchback under ~13% (G28–G29).
- [ ] Routes show only real traffic, never zone-to-zone lines (P16).

**Readability and identity** (`cartography-craft.md`)
- [ ] Each zone's region reads as its own landform and ground, not one repeated texture; the act's signature (the acid pits) dominates.
- [ ] Labels name landforms at a readable size; placeholders are tagged (P12); notes carry what the map can't show (G37).
- [ ] Exaggerations are labelled (G36).

**Studio**
- [ ] Every planned zone has an anchor; bearings are computed; graph checks H7–H11 run, failures reported with options.
- [ ] Camera-side and down-screen cases are flagged (H9–H10).
- [ ] The handoff's facts each name a source; open items are OPEN (H23).

## 2. Checks every map pass runs before it's done

1. **Both eras built from the ops** (`npm run world`, `npm run world before`); the build log has no failed op and no
   `pageErrors`.
2. **Before/after evidence:** frame and full-map screenshots (heightmap and biomes), the 3D views (Azgaar's, and the
   terrain viewer overview and low), compared with the reference (P9, P10).
3. **Lake count** in the frame against the intended list (`list kind:"features" frame`), with subtype, group and name.
4. **Lowest ground:** no basin land at or below the pan; no lake surface below the pan's pools (needs TG1, or a logged
   read-only dev query until it exists).
5. **Drainage:** where every river in the frame ends, per era (needs TG2, or a logged dev query).
6. **Gaea masks** not empty if a biome or group name changed (M9); re-export when terrain or colour changed.
7. **Zone graph** re-run if an anchor, crossing or connection landform moved; drift reported (H6).
8. **`npm run smoke`** if a tool changed; update the registered-tool count when one is added.

A check that couldn't run is listed in the report with the reason, never skipped silently.

## 3. Rubric (self-check and the judge)

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Geography** — the result obeys the physical rules that read at map scale | Breaks a BLOCKING rule (water uphill, a lake not at the low point, a river with no end, a town with no water) | Plausible in parts; major rules unchecked | Rules checked with evidence; minor gaps | Every applicable G-rule checked with map evidence; departures stated with their play reason |
| **Fidelity** — follows the vault and P1–P26; invents no canon | Contradicts a vault decision or invents canon | Follows decisions but fills story gaps silently | Follows decisions; open items mostly marked | Every fact sourced; every gap OPEN with its question; bends only for stated play/look reasons |
| **Usefulness to the studio** — concrete, ranked, actionable in the consumers' terms | Vague advice | Some concrete fixes or facts | Ranked findings with ops, or handoff facts per zone | Ops ready to write; computed bearings; per-zone sheets in level-design and environment-art terms (rule IDs) |
| **Craft and process** (0–1) | Hand edits, one era, no evidence | — | — | Ops not edits, both eras, before/after evidence, sources cited (scored 1) |

Score out of 10. A judge scores lower for a regression and lists every gap ranked and actionable. Exit: 8 or more;
stall rules as in the studio's PIPELINE §6.
